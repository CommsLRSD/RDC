Fresh Food Box Duties

Schedule: Every other Tuesday, 10:00 AM to 11:30 AM

Responsibilities

  • Assist by putting away milk and eggs into the community fridge.
  • Create baskets of vegetables and fruit according to quantities posted by staff.
  • Create up to 52 bins.
  • Break down all boxes and place them into the recycling bin.
  • Sign up in advance.

Volunteer Benefit

By volunteering, you will receive a food box.

Note: Limited to one volunteer slot every two months.

Food Depot Duties

Schedule: Thursday, 10:45 AM to 12:15 PM (weekly)

Responsibilities

  • Set up tables in an "L" shape.
  • Unbag and organize bins with potatoes and onions.
  • Follow the school pickup schedule and distribute potatoes and onions as directed by RDC staff.
  • Assist in bringing bread in from the staff vehicle.
  • Organize bread products into categories.

During Distribution

  • One volunteer counts community members as they move through the line.
  • Two volunteers hand out bread, potatoes, and onions (amounts may vary).
  • Two volunteers replenish bread, potatoes, and onions.

End-of-Day Duties

  • When there are no more community members, tables are returned along the wall.
  • Leftover potatoes, onions, and bread are retained for late arrivals or other community needs and placed in the community food cupboard.

Welcome Area Duties

Requested Time Frame: 10:30 AM to 11:00 AM

Responsibilities

  • Empty the dishwasher if clean.
  • Place all dirty dishes into the dishwasher and start the dishwasher.
  • Wipe down the serving counter with soap and warm water.
  • Check with RDC staff to determine if a new pot of coffee is required (50 cups).
  • Tidy the children's play area and push in chairs.

Ideal For

This role is perfect for individuals who enjoy creating a welcoming environment and helping people feel comfortable.

Clothing Closet Duties

Responsibilities

  • Sort donations and inspect items for damage including holes, rips, tears, and stains.
  • Wash clothing if needed.
  • Check that all zippers are in working order.
  • Place clothing into bins according to type.
  • Once sorting is complete, roll out the three bins and any jackets or dresses hanging on the rack into the hallway and place items in the correct bins.
  • Clean up messy areas and return clothing left out to appropriate bins.
  • Remove unused hangers and rehang pants or jackets that may have fallen off their hangers.
  • Ensure clothing in bins is folded and organized.
  • Lock all lids on bins.
  • Return unused hangers to bins in the staff room.
  • Check the floor and under cupboards to ensure no garbage or items have been left behind.
  • Return the rack and bins to the staff room.
