We offer for community a variety of programs. Community Hub The RDC Community Hub is a welcoming space where community members can access supports, services, learning opportunities, and community activities. Programs and Services Include: Adult learning and skill-building opportunities Community programming and clinics (vision care, flu, tax, dental health, EIA, Service Manitoba, and francophone services) Training programs such as First Aid and Food Handler Certification when applicable Indigenous cultural programming, including Full Moon Ceremonies, Beading & Breakfast and traditional teachings Food Security Supports: Food Depot – Free bread and potatoes every Thursday at noon, come in person and bring your own bag Fresh Food Box – Fresh fruits, vegetables, milk, and eggs for $20 (valued at approximately $40), sign up online or in person using QR Code Emergency Food Hampers – Available for individuals and families in need, call, email or walk-in Other Supports: Free coffee and tea Phone, computer, Wi-Fi, and printing access Clothing closet The Community Hub provides a place to connect, learn, access resources, and build community.
