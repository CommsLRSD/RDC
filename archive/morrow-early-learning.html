About Morrow Early Learning and Child Development 

Morrow Early Learning and Child Development at René Deleurme Site offers child care for infant, toddler and preschool children who live in the communities/catchment areas of René Deleurme Centre, Lavallee, Victor H. L. Wyatt and Victor Mager Schools

René Deleurme Centre site follows the Abecedarian Approach which uses language as the core of early learning and child development. 

The centre is staffed by Early Childhood Educators who provide individualized, relationship-based care that focuses on each child’s individual development. Early Childhood Educators provide respectful, responsive, reciprocal relationships in order to enhance children’s language. Strong language skills allow for greater cognitive and social/emotional development.
