Student Support Services

In Louis Riel School Division, we believe every student should learn in an inclusive environment where they can participate meaningfully in programming that reflects their individual strengths and needs. School teams and divisional staff work closely with students and families to create appropriate educational plans grounded in collaboration and shared decision-making.

Each LRSD school has student services teachers who, alongside school administration and classroom teachers, coordinate supports that help create conditions where all learners can belong, engage, and succeed. Divisional clinicians and specialists further strengthen this work by offering expertise in areas such as speech-language pathology, psychology, social work, occupational therapy, and physiotherapy.

Together, these professionals form a responsive network that supports student growth across academic, social, emotional, behavioural, communicative, functional, and physical domains.

If parents or guardians have concerns about their child’s learning, they are encouraged to begin by speaking with the classroom teacher. The teacher will collaborate as needed with the school’s student services team and other support staff to explore strategies and determine next steps in planning and programming.

Multi-Tiered System of Support (MTSS)

Multi-Tiered System of Supports (MTSS) is a proactive framework that helps schools meet the needs of all students by identifying challenges early and providing the right support. It builds on the Response to Intervention (RTI) model, which uses a tiered approach to give extra help to students who need it. MTSS takes this further by looking at the bigger picture—school-wide practices and barriers that can impact learning for both students and educators. The goal is to create an inclusive, supportive environment for everyone.

In RTI, every student begins with strong, high-quality classroom instruction (Tier 1). If some students need more help, they receive targeted support in small groups (Tier 2). For students with more intensive needs, individualized support is provided (Tier 3)—while keeping them connected to the regular classroom. These supports are flexible and based on each student’s needs, not labels.

Resource Allocation Planning Framework

The Resource Allocation Planning Framework (RAF) outlines how Louis Riel School Division thoughtfully plans and allocates student support in ways that are equitable, transparent, and inclusive. The framework combines professional judgment with clear, shared tools to help schools understand student needs, plan supports, and use resources responsibly. 

Defined scoring approaches describe classroom complexity and overall intensity levels of student support needs, helping identify patterns, align language and approaches, and support equitable decision‑making across the division. Together, the framework supports consistent planning while keeping the focus on student belonging, growth, and increasing independence over time.

LRSD Clinical Services

The services of School Psychologists, School Social Workers, Speech-Language Pathologists, Occupational Therapists and Physiotherapists are available within each school.

These specialists work together with school staff and parents/guardians to support students who may require additional help in order to be successful learners. They may work with individual students, small groups, or provide classroom-based activities.

If you have questions about your child’s learning, physical, social-emotional development or mental health, please start by contacting your classroom teacher.

Our Philosophy

Louis Riel School Division (LRSD) is committed to fostering inclusive and appropriate educational programming for all students. Our educational philosophy is rooted in the belief that every learner deserves to feel accepted, valued, and safe. This is reflected in our divisional vision, mission, and values:

Vision

All members of our community will excel as caring, confident, capable, and resilient life-long learners who contribute to a democratic and sustainable world.

Mission

We provide a safe, inclusive, and engaging environment where personal and collective learning and growth are valued, and each one of us reaches our full potential.

Core Values

  • Shared Responsibility and Collaboration
  • Learning and Well-Becoming
  • Evidence-Informed Decision-Making
  • Equity and Reciprocity
  • Inclusion and Diversity
  • Autonomy

These principles are further supported by Manitoba’s Appropriate Educational Programming Standards, which define inclusion as:

“A way of thinking and acting that allows every individual to feel accepted, valued and safe. An inclusive community consciously evolves to meet the changing needs of its members. Through recognition and support, an inclusive community provides meaningful involvement and equal access to the benefits of citizenship.”
