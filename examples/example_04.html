About the BLAST Program

The BLAST acronym stands for Before-School, Lunch, and After-School Time. While this new program focuses on before- and after-school supervision, "Lunch" is included to reflect our pre-existing commitment to supervising students during the lunch hour in all LRSD schools. Please note that lunch supervision fees are not included in the BLAST program's daily rate. These fees will continue to be handled separately by each school, as in previous years.

A survey conducted by LRSD in Spring 2025 highlighted the need for children to have a safe place to go before and after school. The survey collected 2,936 responses from LRSD families, with over 1,200 respondents indicating an unfulfilled need for programming before and after school. We want to clarify that BLAST is a supervision program for school-aged children, not a childcare service. The LRSD BLAST program aims to support families in accessing supervision during these times and complements the childcare services available in LRSD communities. BLAST programing, which includes arts & crafts, indoor and outdoor play, and social connection before and after school hours, differs from childcare services which are curriculum based and licensed and regulated through the Department of Education and Early Childhood Learning.

Thank you for your understanding and support as we work to enhance student supervision across LRSD. This will be a multi-year project, and school communities will be contacted as we increase capacity and are able to recruit and develop a skilled workforce.

Hours of Operation

7:00 a.m. – morning bell and dismissal bell – 5:30 p.m.

Notes on Hours of Operation:

  • On early dismissal days, BLAST starts one hour earlier.
  • The BLAST program will not be offered on Professional Development/Administration days or holidays.
  • In the event of a return to remote learning, the BLAST program will not be available to students learning remotely. Parents/guardians would not be charged for days their child(ren) are away from the program.

Cost

BLAST program fees are a standard monthly rate and average approximately $15/day. Casual spaces are not available. The cost covers staff salaries, resources, games, activities, supplies, and a small snack for your child in both the morning and the afternoon. This program is not a licensed childcare program and is therefore not eligible for government subsidies. This program is offered by the division on a cost-recovery basis. Note that $10 a day childcare is only available through licensed childcare providers.

Waitlist

A division wide waitlist is now open.

To complete a BLAST waitlist application and be considered for the waitlist, your child must be a registered K-6 LRSD student. 

To apply to the waitlist, please access the 2026/27 BLAST Waitlist Application through the Parent Portal > PowerSchoolSIS > LRSDForms. 

Parents/Guardians with children registered at an LRSD elementary school were sent more information via e-mail on Monday, April 13, 2026.

Parents/Guardians should indicate the earliest possible date they want a spot regardless of whether or not a program exists at their child’s school. This information will assist LRSD in determining which locations to open next.

Note: If your child does not currently have a spot in BLAST you must complete the BLAST Waitlist Application, regardless of whether or not you have requested a spot at your school before.  All previous waitlists have been retired.
