MYConnect

project-based, hands-on learning

MYConnect provides project-based, hands-on learning that reinforces the Grade 7/8 curriculum while strengthening student confidence and community connection.

  • Hosted in woodworking and nutrition lab spaces at RDC and Glenwood School
  • One half day per six-day school cycle
  • Learning includes numeracy, literacy, cooking, shopping, and community-based activities

Schools identify eligible students. For more information contact Lee Foster at 204-257-7308 ext. 63361 or lee.foster@lrsd.net.
