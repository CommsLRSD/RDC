Services Available

Settlement staff support both immediate newcomer needs and long-term community integration.

Programs and Services

Group programming includes English Conversation Circles, sewing, parenting, healthy relationships, and spring/summer break youth wellness camps.

Mentorship

Newly arrived permanent residents are paired with Canadian citizens or long-time community members to build community knowledge and confidence.

Resources and Referrals

  • Employment and housing referrals
  • Education and job training pathways
  • Mental health supports

Languages supported

Amharic, Arabic, Bulgarian, English, French, Lingala, Oromo, Russian, Sango, Somali, and Swahili.

Email: settlement@lrsd.net

Who can access support

Services are designed for newcomer and recently arrived permanent residents seeking settlement guidance and community integration support.

How to connect

Contact the team by email to request referrals, register for group programs, or ask about mentorship and language-specific services.

Program focus

The program strengthens belonging, confidence, and access to essential services through practical, community-based supports.
