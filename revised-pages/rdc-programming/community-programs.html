René Deleurme Centre Programs

Programs and services are developed with community voice and changing needs in mind.

The René Deleurme Centre is a hub where community members can access supports, services, learning opportunities, community activities, and Community Kitchen supports.

Programs and Services

  • Adult learning and skill-building opportunities
  • Community clinics (vision care, flu, tax, dental health, EIA, Service Manitoba, francophone services)
  • Training programs including First Aid and Food Handler Certification (when offered)
  • Indigenous cultural programming (Full Moon Ceremonies, Beading & Breakfast, traditional teachings)
  • ASPIRE: summer learning and recreation opportunities for students
  • Settlement Program: newcomer support, referrals, and community connection

Food Security Supports

  • Food Depot: free bread and potatoes every Thursday at noon
  • Fresh Food Box: fresh produce, milk, and eggs for $20
  • Emergency Food Hampers: available by call, email, or walk-in
  • Community Kitchen meals, food distribution, and volunteer opportunities

Other Supports

  • Free coffee and tea
  • Phone, computer, Wi-Fi, and printing access
  • Clothing closet

Community Kitchen

The LRSD Community Kitchen is a hands-on, full-service commissary and community kitchen that provides meals for students in Louis Riel School Division and the surrounding community. The program supports people experiencing food insecurity while creating opportunities to build life and workplace skills.

Volunteer contact: Chef Michael Erin — 204-257-7308

Kitchen Volunteer Roles

Schedules can shift based on meal production and distribution needs.

Fresh Food Box

Every other Tuesday, 10:00 AM – 11:30 AM

  • Prepare produce bins and support fridge storage
  • Build up to 52 boxes
  • Break down and recycle boxes

Food Depot

Thursdays, 10:45 AM – 12:15 PM

  • Set up distribution area and organize food
  • Support line flow and replenishment
  • Clean and reset space after distribution

Welcome Area

Suggested time: 10:30 AM – 11:00 AM

  • Maintain serving and coffee area
  • Support dishwashing and tidying
  • Help create a welcoming environment

Clothing Closet

Flexible / assigned

  • Sort and check donated clothing
  • Organize bins and racks
  • Restore and clean volunteer area
