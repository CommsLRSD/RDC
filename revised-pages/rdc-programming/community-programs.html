Star People: Stitching Together Community

At René Deleurme Centre, sewing became a shared language that brought together Indigenous, newcomer, and non-Indigenous community members to create a meaningful Star Blanket.

The finished blanket includes tributes to residential school survivors and children who did not return home, and reflects collaboration, relationship, and healing through community creation.

René Deleurme Centre Community Programs

The Louis Riel School Division operates the René Deleurme Centre, and RDC community programs continue to grow through community voice, partnerships, and shared learning.

The René Deleurme Centre is a hub where community members can access supports, services, learning opportunities, food security programs, and community activities in one place.

Programs and Services

  • Adult learning and skill-building opportunities
  • Community clinics including vision care, flu, tax, dental health, EIA, Service Manitoba, and francophone services
  • Training opportunities such as First Aid and Food Handler Certification when available
  • Indigenous cultural programming including Full Moon Ceremonies, Beading & Breakfast, and traditional teachings

Food Security Supports

  • Food Depot with free bread and potatoes every Thursday at noon
  • Fresh Food Box orders with produce, milk, and eggs
  • Emergency food hampers available by call, email, or walk-in
  • Drop-in access to coffee, tea, computers, Wi-Fi, printing, and the clothing closet

Community Kitchen

The Community Kitchen is part of RDC's day-to-day community programming, providing meals for students in Louis Riel School Division and supporting community members experiencing food insecurity.

The kitchen also creates opportunities to build life and workplace skills through volunteer roles and hands-on participation.

Programs delivered through RDC and Louis Riel School Division

ASPIRE and the Settlement Program are run through the René Deleurme Centre and Louis Riel School Division, with division staff supporting daily operations at RDC.

ASPIRE

Academic Summer Program Including Recreation and Education supports student learning with camp-style summer programming focused on literacy, numeracy, problem solving, and social development.

Settlement Program

The Settlement Program supports newcomers with community connections, referrals, group programming, and practical guidance as they build belonging in the LRSD community.
