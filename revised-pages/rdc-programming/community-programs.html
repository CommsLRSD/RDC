Community Hub

Programs and services are developed with community voice and changing needs in mind.

The RDC Community Hub is a welcoming space where community members can access supports, services, learning opportunities, and community activities.

Programs and Services

  • Adult learning and skill-building opportunities
  • Community clinics (vision care, flu, tax, dental health, EIA, Service Manitoba, francophone services)
  • Training programs including First Aid and Food Handler Certification (when offered)
  • Indigenous cultural programming (Full Moon Ceremonies, Beading & Breakfast, traditional teachings)

Food Security Supports

  • Food Depot: free bread and potatoes every Thursday at noon
  • Fresh Food Box: fresh produce, milk, and eggs for $20
  • Emergency Food Hampers: available by call, email, or walk-in

Other Supports

  • Free coffee and tea
  • Phone, computer, Wi-Fi, and printing access
  • Clothing closet

Star People: Stitching Together Community

At René Deleurme Centre, sewing became a shared language that brought together Indigenous, newcomer, and non-Indigenous community members to create a meaningful Star Blanket.

The finished blanket includes tributes to residential school survivors and children who did not return home, and reflects collaboration, relationship, and healing through community creation.
