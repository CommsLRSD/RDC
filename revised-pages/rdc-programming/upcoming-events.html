Fresh Food Box

Cost: $20 per box (about $40 value)

Contents: Assorted fruit, vegetables, 2 L milk, and 1 dozen eggs (no substitutions)

Order deadline: Sign up by 9:00 a.m. Tuesday the week before pickup; order each time

Limits: 1 box per household; LRSD catchment families get priority

Payment: Cash, debit, or credit card at pickup

Pickup locations and time:

  • Every second Tuesday: RDC, 511 St. Anne's Rd, 2:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.
  • Every second Tuesday: Nelson McIntyre Collegiate, 188 St. Mary's Rd, 3:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.
  • School breaks: earlier pickup times at RDC only

Contact: 204-257-7308

Order: Order a Fresh Food Box
