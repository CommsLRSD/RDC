Fresh Food Box

  • $20 per box, about $40 in value
  • Includes assorted fruit, vegetables, 2 L milk, and 1 dozen eggs; no substitutions
  • Sign up by 9:00 a.m. Tuesday the week before pickup; order each time
  • Limit 1 box per household; LRSD catchment families get priority
  • Pay by cash, debit, or credit card at pickup
  • Every second Tuesday: RDC, 511 St. Anne's Rd, 2:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.
  • Every second Tuesday: Nelson McIntyre Collegiate, 188 St. Mary's Rd, 3:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.
  • School breaks: earlier pickup times at RDC only
  • Questions: 204-257-7308
