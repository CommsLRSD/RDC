EDGE Programs

EDGE program information now lives on the combined Community Partners Programs page.

EDGE supports newcomer and adult learners through ESL, LINC, and literacy programming delivered in partnership with the René Deleurme Centre community.

EDGE ESL (LINC)

  • Part-time language learning for newcomer adults
  • Canadian Language Benchmark and PBLA-aligned instruction
  • Care for Newcomer Children available for eligible families

EDGE Literacy

  • Reading, writing, speaking, listening, and employability skill development
  • Continuous intake and free participation
  • Morning and afternoon learning options
