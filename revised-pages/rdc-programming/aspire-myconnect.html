ASPIRE and MYConnect

This page combines two student learning programs offered through RDC.

ASPIRE (Academic Summer Program Including Recreation and Education)

ASPIRE is an equity-based summer program that helps students continue learning through engaging educational activities and camp-style experiences.

  • Focuses on math, reading, writing, problem solving, and social development
  • Led by high-energy instructors in the Faculty of Education
  • No cost to participating families

Registration begins in mid-May. Families should contact school administration for participation details and school eligibility.

MYConnect

MYConnect provides project-based, hands-on learning that reinforces the Grade 7/8 curriculum while strengthening student confidence and community connection.

  • Hosted in woodworking and nutrition lab spaces at RDC and Glenwood School
  • One half day per six-day school cycle
  • Learning includes numeracy, literacy, cooking, shopping, and community-based activities

Schools identify eligible students. For more information contact Lee Foster at 204-257-7308 ext. 63361 or lee.foster@lrsd.net.
