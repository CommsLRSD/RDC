About the Community Kitchen

Programs and supports are open to all community members.

The LRSD Community Kitchen is a hands-on, full-service commissary and community kitchen that provides meals for students in the Louis Riel School Division and the surrounding community.

The program supports community members experiencing food insecurity while offering opportunities to develop life skills and gain real-world workplace experience.

Whether you are retired, a student, or anywhere in between, lots of opportunities are available to sign up as a volunteer!
