Community Kitchen

Hands-on commissary kitchen supporting food security and skill-building in LRSD

The LRSD Community Kitchen is a hands-on, full-service commissary and community kitchen that provides meals for students in Louis Riel School Division and the surrounding community.

The program supports people experiencing food insecurity while creating opportunities to build life and workplace skills.

Volunteer contact: Chef Michael Erin — michael.erin@lrsd.net — 204-257-7308

Volunteer Roles

Fresh Food Box

Schedule: Every other Tuesday, 10:00 AM to 11:30 AM

  • Prepare produce bins and support fridge storage
  • Build up to 52 boxes
  • Break down and recycle boxes

Food Depot

Schedule: Thursdays, 10:45 AM to 12:15 PM

  • Set up distribution area and organize food
  • Support line flow and replenishment
  • Clean and reset space after distribution

Welcome Area

Suggested time: 10:30 AM to 11:00 AM

  • Maintain serving and coffee area
  • Support dishwashing and tidying
  • Help create a welcoming environment

Clothing Closet

Schedule: Flexible / assigned

  • Sort and check donated clothing
  • Organize bins and racks
  • Restore and clean volunteer area

Note: schedules can shift based on meal production and distribution needs.
