Hands-on food security and volunteer opportunities at René Deleurme Centre

Community Kitchen

The Community Kitchen supports Louis Riel School Division students and the wider RDC community through food access, meal preparation, and volunteer learning.

The Community Kitchen is a full-service commissary and community kitchen based at René Deleurme Centre. It provides meals, food distribution support, and practical skill-building opportunities for community members and volunteers.
