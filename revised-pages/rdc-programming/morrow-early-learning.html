Morrow Early Learning and Child Development

Language-rich, relationship-based early learning and child care at René Deleurme Centre

RDC is located on Treaty 1 Territory, the traditional lands of Anishinaabe, Ininewak, Cree, Oji-Cree, Dakota, and Dene Peoples, and in the homeland of the Métis Nation. We acknowledge the Treaties and commit to moving forward in reconciliation and partnership.

We also acknowledge Shoal Lake, Treaty 3 Territory, as the source of the water that sustains our lives.

Program Overview

The program emphasizes language-rich, relationship-based early learning experiences.

Morrow Early Learning and Child Development at René Deleurme Site offers child care for infant, toddler, and preschool children who live in the René Deleurme, Lavallee, Victor H. L. Wyatt, and Victor Mager school communities.

The site follows the Abecedarian Approach, using language as a core of early learning. Early Childhood Educators provide individualized, relationship-based care to support cognitive, social, and emotional development.
