About René Deleurme Centre

The René Deleurme Centre (RDC) is a community learning centre in Louis Riel School Division. RDC includes community stakeholders such as newcomer settlement services, family outreach, daycare, adult literacy, and adult EAL programming. Programming is equity-based and responsive to community needs.

Code of Conduct

All staff, students, caregivers, community members, and occupants are expected to treat one another with respect and support the growth and learning of everyone who accesses RDC services.

  1. Respect and dignity
  2. Commitment to learning and success
  3. Integrity and honesty
  4. Safety and well-being
  5. Collaboration and teamwork
  6. Confidentiality and privacy
  7. Professionalism
  8. Responsibility and accountability
